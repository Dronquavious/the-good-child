Research notes

1697 · Charles Perrault

The cakes, butter, mill, and two roads come from the tale. Mother sends Red on an errand. The warning about the danger of apparently gentle wolves comes from Perrault’s final moral. Source citation

1857 · Jacob and Wilhelm Grimm

Mother’s rules concern the path and proper manners. The first wolf is followed by a second encounter in which Grandmother and Red work together. Source citation

Original modern remix inspired by Thurber and Dahl

The published parodies let Red defeat the wolf. The lantern, footprints, and nonviolent responses are original to this game. Primary sources
